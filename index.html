Our Story

A table that feels like home

Hawa Restaurant & Party Centre sits in the heart of Mymensingh, near Dhaka Bypass on Maskanda Road — the city's favourite stop for family dinners, friend hangouts and big celebrations.

Every dish is cooked with fresh, hand-ground masala. From our signature Beef Roast to tender Chicken Korma, spicy Masala Bhuna and the season's fresh Hilsa fry, flavour and quality are never compromised.

“We came with the whole family — the ambience and food quality amazed us. The Beef Roast is truly exceptional!” — Sohel Rana, regular guest

Signature Dishes

What Mymensingh comes for

Kacchi Biryani & Rice Platters

Fragrant basmati rice slow-cooked with marinated mutton, saffron and fried onions — served the traditional Bangladeshi way.

From the Grill

Chicken tikka, beef seekh kebab and sizzling platters straight off the charcoal grill, with fresh green chutney and onions.

🥩 Beef Roast

Spicy, slow-cooked beef — the house favourite that keeps meat lovers coming back.

🍗 Chicken Korma

Tender chicken in a golden, creamy masala gravy. Mild, rich and comforting.

🌶️ Masala Bhuna

A fiery, deeply spiced meat dish for those who like real heat.

🐟 Ilish (Hilsa) Fry

Bangladesh's national fish, fried crisp with mustard — best in season.

🥘 Chinese & Thai

Fried rice, noodles, sizzling dishes and Thai curries alongside the classics.

🍰 Desserts & Drinks
