# Food-led revision — 2 November 2026

## Reason for revision
The user said the previous deployed version was worse and explicitly requested generated food images. Its oval-cropped photograph, bare geometric table and extended scroll runway weakened the appetite and pacing. This iteration gives the food, typography and functional menu the visual weight.

## Current sequence
1. A whole brass biryani platter on deep emerald, beside a bold Follow the aroma headline. Warm texture, soft shadow, subtle pointer/scroll movement; menu and gathering links immediately available.
2. Saffron category strip leads into three appetising, distinct featured dishes. Whole biryani, mixed grill and beef roast are separate assets. Each has details and direct add-to-request controls.
3. An oversized terracotta firni composition shifts to warm cream and sage, with a concise dessert invitation.
4. Gathering enquiry uses a food-sharing composition and direct contact. No fabricated restaurant interior.
5. Address, phone, directions and menu finish the page. Existing business details are carried forward.

## Motion decision
Ordinary scrolling and small responsive parallax replace the earlier pinned table sequence. The bare Three.js scene has been removed from the active page. This revision does not implement a fully rotatable food model or a platter-to-table 3D transition. Generated food is a layered bitmap. This tradeoff prioritizes the user's request for a stronger-looking site and real appetite; suitable multiview food assets remain necessary for believable full 3D.

## Mobile composition
Centered two-line headline, visible platter and primary action, followed by full-width dish imagery. Intermediate mobile widths cap the platter to keep actions clear. Search/filter controls remain compact; category strip scrolls within its own container. Native dialogs support keyboard and touch.

## Provenance
Four images were created with the built-in image-generation tool after explicit user authorization. Prompts are in assets/GENERATED-FOOD-PROMPTS.md. All generated images are illustrative; no actual interior, chef, customer or restaurant history was invented. Current serving presentation and prices should be confirmed with Hawa.
